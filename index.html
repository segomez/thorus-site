Hi, I’m Sebastián Gómez, founder of Thorus Consulting.

For more than twenty years I’ve worked between business owners and the technology people they hire. I’ve seen the same problems show up again and again, in companies of every size.

If you’re on this page, there’s a good chance you’re dealing with one of three situations.

One: you invested in a digital transformation project and it’s going sideways. It’s late, it’s over budget, or it simply isn’t delivering what you were promised.

Two: you suspect you’ve been oversold. You’re paying for software, licenses or a platform that is bigger, more complex and more expensive than your business needs.

Or three: you’re about to choose a technology partner, a software vendor, an agency or a development firm, and you want to get that decision right before you sign.

Below this video you’ll find one button for each situation. Pick the one that sounds most like you. I’ll explain why it happens, what most people don’t consider, and what to think about before your next move.
